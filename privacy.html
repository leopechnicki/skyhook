SKYHOOK - PRIVACY POLICY

Effective 25 September 2026. Applies to the SKYHOOK game on the web (skyhookplay.com) and the SKYHOOK Android app (com.leopechnicki.skyhook). Developer: Leonardo Pechnicki dos Santos, Krakow, Poland.

SKYHOOK is a one-touch arcade game. It is fully playable without an account, without an internet connection, and without telling us anything. This page lists exactly what the game keeps on your device, what it sends to our server if you choose to create an account, and how to delete it all.

1. The short version

  • No ads, no ad networks, no trackers, no analytics. The Android app shows no advertisements and does not start any advertising SDK. It does not read your advertising ID.
  • No account is required. Your high score and your ship colours are saved only on your device.
  • If you create an account we store your email address, a password hash, the public username you choose, and your runs (score, hooks, altitude, duration and the input timing of each run, which is used only to detect bots on the leaderboard).
  • We never sell or share any of it. The only third party that processes it is our database host, Supabase.
  • You can have your account deleted by emailing us from the address you signed up with (section 7); the account, the username, the runs and the saved ship colours all go with it. An in-game delete button is planned and this page will say so when it ships.

2. Data that stays on your device

The game uses the browser's (or the Android WebView's) local storage for:

WhatWhy
Your best scoreSo the title screen can show it.
Your ship colours (nose, window, body, fire)So your paint job survives a restart. Stored as four colour codes.
Whether sound is muted, and whether you have finished the tutorialSo the game does not ask again.
Your sign-in session, if you signed inSo you stay signed in. Removed when you sign out.
A run waiting to be submitted, if you were offlineSo a score is not lost when the network is. Sent the next time you are online and signed in.

None of this is sent to us. If you have Android's app backup switched on, Android may include the app's local data in your own Google backup, as it does for any app that allows backup; we never see that backup. Uninstalling the app or clearing the site's data removes all of it.

3. Data sent to our server (only with an account)

The optional global leaderboard is hosted on Supabase (project region: EU, Frankfurt). No personal data is sent until you tap Sign in / create account. Opening the leaderboard as a guest only requests the public top list from that server; like any web request it shows the server your IP address for the moment it is served, and the game stores nothing about it.

DataWhenWhat it is used for
Email address and passwordCreating an account or signing in Signing you in and sending the confirmation and password-reset emails. The password is stored only as a hash by Supabase Auth. The email is never shown to other players and is not copied into any other table.

Like any web server, Supabase's infrastructure records the IP address of each request in short-lived operational logs. We do not use those logs to identify players.

4. What we do not collect

  • No advertising identifier, no device identifier, no fingerprinting.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDK. No Google Analytics, no Firebase.
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files. The Android app declares INTERNET (the leaderboard) and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, plus WAKE_LOCK and FOREGROUND_SERVICE, which are pulled in by a bundled Google library that the game never starts. None of these is a runtime permission and none gives access to your data.
  • No purchases. The app contains no in-app products.
  • No data from anyone who does not create an account, beyond the device-local items in section 2.

5. Children

SKYHOOK is a general-audience game with no violence, no chat between players and no ads. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. Creating an account requires an email address; if a child has created one, the parent or guardian can delete it as described in section 7 and we will remove it.

6. Sharing and retention

Account data is stored by Supabase on our behalf and by nobody else. We do not sell, rent or share it with advertisers or data brokers. The leaderboard shows only usernames and scores. Data is kept for as long as your account exists and is deleted with it. A run held for bot review is kept in the ledger and simply not shown on the public board until it is cleared. The developer may suspend or delete an account that breaks the leaderboard rules (for example, automated play); such actions are recorded in an audit log with the username and reason so they can be reviewed.

7. Deleting your account and data

Email leopsantos@hotmail.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject "SKYHOOK account deletion". We delete the account within 14 days and confirm by email. Deleting the account removes your email address, your username, every run you submitted and your saved ship colours from our server, permanently. There is no partial deletion: the runs cannot stay on the board without the account they belong to. You do not need to delete the app first, and you can create a new account afterwards.

The game does not yet have a delete-account button of its own. When it does, this section will describe it; the email route above stays available either way.

To remove the device-local data in section 2, uninstall the app or clear the site's storage in your browser.

8. Your rights

If you are in the EU/EEA or UK you have the right to access, correct, export and erase your personal data, and to object to or restrict its processing. Email the address in section 7. Our legal basis for processing account data is the contract you enter by creating the account; for the bot-detection telemetry it is our legitimate interest in keeping the leaderboard honest.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published at this address with a new effective date. The source of this page lives in the game's public repository next to the code it describes.
